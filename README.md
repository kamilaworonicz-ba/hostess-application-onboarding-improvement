# Field App Onboarding & Support Optimization

### Business Analysis & Project Delivery Case Study

`Status: Implemented` `Type: Real-World Case Study` `Domain: Retail / Field Operations`  
`Methods: Problem Analysis / Requirements / User Stories / Gherkin / Traceability`

A real onboarding improvement for a tablet application used by approximately **150 field hostesses and 12 Team Leaders across Poland**.

New hostesses often learned to use the application during their first independent shifts. This led to repeated support questions and occasional training activity in the production environment. I identified the problem and led the introduction of a separate training application where hostesses could practice the core workflow before field work.

## 👩‍💼 My Role

I took the improvement from the initial problem to implementation:

- Identified recurring onboarding and support issues.
- Raised the need with my manager and discussed an internal budget option in case the client could not fund the work.
- Agreed on the estimated cost and timeline with our developer.
- Presented the solution to the client, who supported it and allocated a budget.
- Coordinated development and tested the training application with several Team Leaders before launch.

The training application was deployed **within one month of agreeing the details with the client**.

## 🛠️ What Was Implemented

A separate training version of the hostess application allowed new users to practice its core workflow without creating training records in the production survey data.

## 📈 Observed Outcome

After implementation, support requests from newly onboarded hostesses **decreased noticeably**. Based on day-to-day observation, a new hostess who previously might have contacted support around **10 times in her first week** typically needed **no more than 3 contacts** after the training application was introduced.

These figures are operational observations, not the results of a formal before-and-after study.

## 🎯 Scope

**In scope:**
- Onboarding process design (training environment) for new hostesses, up to their first independent field shift
- Recording training completion data in the database for future reporting and audit purposes

**Out of scope:**
- FAQ authoring (already exists as an input)
- In-app Team Leader visibility of training completion status
- Automatic blocking of production access for hostesses who have not completed the training
